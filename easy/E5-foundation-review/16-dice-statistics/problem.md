# Dice Statistics

**Level:** E5 · **Type:** Application
**Concepts:** arrays, loops, arithmetic, formatting

## Prompt
Read five dice values and print their sum, minimum, maximum, and average.

## Input
Five integers.

## Output
Four lines with average shown to one decimal place.

## Examples

### Example 1
Input:
```text
1 2 3 4 5
```
Output:
```text
Sum: 15
Min: 1
Max: 5
Average: 3.0
```

### Example 2
Input:
```text
6 6 6 6 6
```
Output:
```text
Sum: 30
Min: 6
Max: 6
Average: 6.0
```

### Example 3
Input:
```text
6 1 4 2 3
```
Output:
```text
Sum: 16
Min: 1
Max: 6
Average: 3.2
```

## Constraints and edge cases
Follow the stated input constraints and match output text, capitalization, spacing, and decimal precision exactly.

## Hint
Initialize min and max from the first value.
