# High Score

**Level:** E5 · **Type:** Application
**Concepts:** conditionals, comparisons

## Prompt
Print the highest of three scores. Print `New high score!` on a second line only when the third score is strictly highest.

## Input
Three integers: score1, score2, score3.

## Output
Highest score, then an optional announcement.

## Examples

### Example 1
Input:
```text
10 20 30
```
Output:
```text
30
New high score!
```

### Example 2
Input:
```text
30 20 10
```
Output:
```text
30
```

### Example 3
Input:
```text
10 30 30
```
Output:
```text
30
```

## Constraints and edge cases
Follow the stated input constraints and match output text, capitalization, spacing, and decimal precision exactly.

## Hint
Keep the third-score test strict.
