# Damage Calculator

**Level:** E5 · **Type:** Application
**Concepts:** `int`, conditionals, arithmetic

## Prompt
Read attack and defense, then print damage. Damage is at least 1.

## Input
Two integers: attack and defense.

## Output
`Damage: X`

## Examples

### Example 1
Input:
```text
10 3
```
Output:
```text
Damage: 7
```

### Example 2
Input:
```text
5 10
```
Output:
```text
Damage: 1
```

### Example 3
Input:
```text
8 7
```
Output:
```text
Damage: 1
```

## Constraints and edge cases
Follow the stated input constraints and match output text, capitalization, spacing, and decimal precision exactly.

## Hint
Compare the subtraction with 1.
